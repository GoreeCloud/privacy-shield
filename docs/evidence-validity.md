# Privacy Shield Evidence Validity

A current Privacy Shield `protected` state or `production_approved: true` status requires a producer- or applicable-policy-defined validity deadline.

`valid_until` identifies how long the status evidence may support the current privacy claim. Missing or expired required validity fails closed. Consumers, including GoreeCloud Mesh, may evaluate this deadline but may not extend, replace, or override it.

This contract preserves Privacy Shield authority for privacy controls, data minimization, retention expectations, privacy capability governance, and privacy status. It does not create runtime acceptance, production approval, deployment authorization, release authorization, or Stable qualification by itself.
