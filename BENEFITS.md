# GoreeCloud Privacy Shield — Benefits

Privacy Shield is designed to improve GoreeCloud privacy without centralizing unnecessary private activity. The benefits below are supportable only within the capability and runtime scope that is actually implemented and accepted.

## Consistent privacy authority

Shared contracts and capability names give GoreeCloud applications a common way to express privacy responsibilities while leaving execution with the component that actually performs the work.

## Purpose- and operation-scoped decisions

The Privacy Shield 2.0 authorization model is designed to bind authority to a specific operation, purpose, resource, destination, processing zone, retention posture, and other applicable constraints rather than treating authentication alone as permission for arbitrary data use.

## Data minimization

Privacy Shield prefers local-first processing, minimized status, minimized evidence, and least-scope access. It explicitly rejects collecting raw private activity merely to make dashboards or status displays more detailed.

## Conservative status

Unknown, stale, unavailable, degraded, incompatible, or unsupported evidence remains visible as such instead of being silently converted into a favorable protection state.

## Runtime accountability

Per-runtime and per-capability acceptance reduces the risk that one successful integration is misrepresented as proof for the entire platform.

## User control and explainability

Where a privacy behavior is user-configurable, Privacy Shield requires understandable scope and truthful controls. Planned 2.0 work expands consent lifecycle, decision explanations, receipts, preview, and Privacy Lock; those planned capabilities are not presented here as already production available.

## Separation of concerns

Privacy Shield governs privacy and data-use concerns while Wardveil Security governs security/protection, Everkeep governs resilience/recovery, GoreeCloud Identity governs identity/authentication, and individual runtimes retain their technical authority. This prevents a single subsystem from silently inheriting unrelated authority.

## Browser privacy foundation

For the Browser runtime, Privacy Shield provides a shared model for native tracking resistance, request blocking, tracking-parameter cleanup, local-resource substitution, exceptions, and privacy status while preserving Firefox/Gecko security boundaries.

A listed benefit must not be converted into a production claim without the acceptance evidence required for the exact runtime and capability.
