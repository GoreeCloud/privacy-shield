# GoreeCloud Privacy Shield — Competitive Objectives

This document records product objectives and differentiation targets. It does not claim that every objective is already implemented or that Privacy Shield is superior to a named third-party product.

## Primary objectives

Privacy Shield should differentiate GoreeCloud privacy architecture by pursuing:

- durable authorization that travels with the operation rather than relying only on requester identity;
- explicit purpose limitation and least-scope authorization;
- local-first processing and aggressive data minimization;
- privacy-safe status and evidence rather than centralized raw activity collection;
- explainable allow/deny/constrained/user-decision outcomes;
- independently accepted runtime capabilities rather than a misleading global protection badge;
- fail-closed behavior when required authority/evidence is stale, missing, conflicting, malformed, or unavailable;
- user-controlled consent, revocation, exceptions, and privacy restrictions;
- auditable but minimized evidence that can show why a privacy decision was made without exposing unnecessary private payloads;
- strong separation among privacy, security, identity, networking, and resilience authorities;
- first-party GoreeCloud Browser privacy controls that preserve upstream Firefox/Gecko security boundaries;
- consistent Glaze UI presentation and an unmistakable Privacy Shield identity.

## Evaluation dimensions

Future product comparisons should use evidence rather than marketing summaries. Useful dimensions include:

- amount and sensitivity of data required to operate the privacy system;
- whether privacy enforcement happens locally or requires remote observation;
- purpose/permission granularity;
- consent expiration/revocation behavior;
- protection against authority widening;
- runtime-specific proof and freshness;
- explainability and user agency;
- failure-state truthfulness;
- privacy of logs, telemetry, diagnostics, and evidence;
- recovery/retention/deletion correctness;
- accessibility and interaction quality;
- portability across GoreeCloud applications and runtimes.

## Non-objectives

Privacy Shield should not compete by maximizing telemetry, centralizing all private traffic, replacing Wardveil Security or GoreeCloud Identity, claiming universal capability coverage, or presenting source validation as production protection.

Competitive objectives must remain synchronized with verified project direction and must be revised when evidence or architecture changes.
