## Security and privacy boundary

Authorization travels with the data operation, not merely with caller identity. Authentication establishes who a caller is; Privacy Shield separately determines whether a specific operation is authorized under purpose, resource, operation, processing-zone, destination, retention, freshness, revocation, replay, and applicable consent/policy constraints.

Signed Privacy Shield capabilities and signing keys remain authority-local. Consumer-facing transports should use opaque references plus independently verified service credentials. Failure, ambiguity, stale evidence, malformed identity or operation-bound claim data, unsupported contract versions, unavailable authority, or unverifiable production evidence must fail closed without exposing signing material, raw capability claims, reusable credentials, or sensitive internal state.
